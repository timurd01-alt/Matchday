How it works

Three steps, the same way every game.

  1. 01

    Predict

    Matchday's own ratings engine produces a win probability for every priced game.

  2. 02

    Compare

    Each probability sits beside the market's number, so you can see where the two disagree.

  3. 03

    Grade

    Picks lock before kickoff and are graded after the result. Nothing locked is ever rewritten.
