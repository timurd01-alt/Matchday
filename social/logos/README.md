# Team logo assets

These team marks are used for identification in Matchday's editorial social graphics.
They were collected from the public `klunn91/team-logos` NCAA library and remain the
property of their respective schools and trademark owners. Their inclusion here does
not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

The renderer maps team names to these local files in `social/weekly-data.js` and falls
back to a generated monogram whenever a team has not yet been mapped.
