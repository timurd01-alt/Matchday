# Team logo assets

These team marks are used for identification in Matchday's game, results, ranking, and
editorial social-graphics interfaces.
They were collected from the public `klunn91/team-logos` NCAA library and remain the
property of their respective schools and trademark owners. Their inclusion here does
not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

The renderers map team names to these local files and fall back to a generated monogram
whenever a team has not yet been mapped.
