What failed the introduction?

The retained non-MLB fixture export names Arsenal FC v Coventry City FC for August 21, but marks its 79% model lean as having no market to compare against. The configured data also has no official, append-only market-snapshot ledger for a fixture-safe audit. The only frozen forecast ledger on hand is MLB, which this edition excludes.

Legacy odds cache files do not repair that gap. They cannot prove a current fixture identity, reliable home/away orientation, or contributing-book count; the latest NFL cache is from July 17, and several soccer cache files are from July. A market with unknown coverage and stale timing is not a weaker consensus. It is a different object.

Could the model factors rescue it?

No. Rating, recent form, home advantage, schedule context, and competition adjustments can explain why a model leans, but they cannot verify what a current market thinks. Rating and form also overlap enough that treating them as independent confirmation would double-count some of the same story. We keep the factors as context, not as a substitute market.
