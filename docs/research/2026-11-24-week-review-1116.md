# Week review: 16–22 November 2026

Written 2026-11-24. This is a review of the published record. It changes nothing.

## Sources and method

- **Graded record:** `trdlab_picks.json` → `scorecard.sports.<sport>.recent` on
  `origin/main` at `f25185f` (2026-11-24). The week runs Monday to Sunday,
  16–22 Nov, by tip-off time in US Central (UTC−6). There are 72 graded football
  picks, 352 graded basketball picks (1 still pending), and the season totals
  through 22 Nov.
- `probability_pct` is the published probability at the lock, which is 60
  minutes before the start. `market_probability_pct` is the no-vig consensus
  stored in the same lock.
- **Calibration:** "Expected wins" is the sum of the published probabilities.
- **Home and away:** read from the "Away at Home" event name. Neutral sites that
  are written with "at" are counted as home or away, so the split is a little
  noisy.
- **Experiment status:** `python -m scripts.ncaam_early_season_experiment --db
  C:/TRDLabData/trdlab.sqlite3`, run read-only on 2026-11-24 at 18:51 UTC.
- **Odds supply:** `odds_quota`, `fetch_state` and `market_snapshots` in
  `C:\TRDLabData\trdlab.sqlite3`, opened read-only.

### Football: the best record of the year, and still behind the market

- Football went 54–18, about 2 wins better than its stated probabilities. The
  hit rate follows the slate, though: the expected rate was 72.4% against 68.4%
  the week before. A good record here is a schedule effect, not a better model.
- On the same 66 priced games the market beat the model by 0.0125 Brier. The
  model has trailed the market by Brier in 5 of the last 6 weeks. The gaps were
  +0.0101, +0.0044 and +0.0125 in the weeks of 2, 9 and 16 November.
- The losses were losses the market shared. Penn State (91.7%, market 92.8%),
  Boise State (90.1% / 86.0%), Duke (81.0% / 79.3%) and Florida State
  (73.4% / 75.7%) were all favourites in both views.
- The biggest model–market gaps all ran the same way. The model was 7 to 11
  points less confident than the market on road favourites, and those
  favourites won: Wisconsin, Iowa, Miami (OH), Houston and Texas Tech. Since 9
  November, road picks are +2.2 wins over expectation (n=60). On priced games
  the model sits 1.9 points below the market on those picks. The sample is
  small, so act on nothing yet.
- **Season-long, the model's departures from the price carry no information.**
  On priced picks:

  | Model minus market on our side | n | Hit | Model said | Market said |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | more than +3 pts | 189 | 60.3% | 72.2% | 62.3% |
  | within 3 pts | 290 | 75.5% | 74.5% | 74.8% |
  | less than −3 pts | 193 | 74.1% | 66.8% | 74.2% |

  Wherever the model and the market differ by more than 3 points, the market's
  number is the calibrated one, in both directions. This confirms the
  2026-11-10 standing (`model-vs-market` notes) and does not change it. The
  model picked against the market twice all week and went 1–1. Its season
  record against the market is 18–34.

### Basketball: the worst week so far, concentrated in two places

- Basketball won 259 games against 272.8 expected, a shortfall of 13.8, and its
  Brier rose from 0.132 to 0.152. The top of the range held: 90%+ picks went
  114–0.
- **Away picks:** 44–37 against 53.2 expected (−9.2). The season figure is
  −19.7 on 182 picks. Home picks are −4.8 on 262. The shortfall grows each
  week: −4.1, −5.0, then −8.9 over the last three weeks. On priced away picks
  the model is 6.2 points more confident than the market. Early in the season
  the model overrates road teams, which means it underrates home court. That
  is the effect the 2026-11-10 study found.
- **Coin flips:** picks rated 50–60% went 30–42 (41.7%). Over the season this
  band hits 47.6%. A pick the model calls close is currently worse than a coin.
- **Overconfident middle:** 70–80% picks hit 69% and 80–90% picks hit 79%.
- **The experiment does not yet fix this.** `ncaam-early-season-calibration-v1`
  has 321 of its 600 games. The candidate is slightly worse than production:
  paired Brier +0.0029, 95% CI −0.0019 to +0.0102. On the 103 priced games in
  the cohort, the candidate scores 0.161, production 0.154 and the market 0.134.
  The candidate makes two changes at once: it adds 2.83 home points and it
  stretches every margin by 1.264. The home points point the right way. The
  stretch makes favourites more confident, and this season the 70–90% bands are
  already too confident. The terms are sealed, so leave v1 to finish.
  A home-points-only variant would have to be a new pre-registered
  declaration. It would be scored only on games after it is registered,
  because this week was used to form the idea.

## Why last week's basketball market comparison is mostly missing

The basketball "vs market" figure for the week covers only 47 of 352 games. By
day: 16 Nov 12/33, 17 Nov 35/65, and 0 priced from 18 to 22 Nov.

1. **The Odds API credits ran out.** `odds_quota` reads 22 remaining with 478
   used, below the engine's 25-credit `RESERVE`. That pauses paid `/odds`
   calls, and the last basketball pull from that source was 17 Nov. The month
   spent about 28 credits a day against roughly 16 a day that would be
   sustainable. The cause is in `trdlab/odds_budget.py`. It divides spendable
   credits by the days left, but it applies that number as the cap for one
   poller cycle (`credits_this_cycle`), not for one day. The poller runs every
   10 minutes, so the "daily" allowance never limits a day. This is the same
   gap AGENTS.md describes for the site's providers, where a reserve keeps the
   balance off zero but does not ration the month.
2. **The free fallback did collect lines, but the scorecard ignores them.**
   `espn_odds` snapshots covered 87–144 basketball games a day from 18 to 21
   Nov. Forecasts read both providers (`forecasting.py`, `paper.py`). The
   scorecard's `_identity`, `_market_at` and the twin lookup read only
   `provider='the_odds_api'` (`trdlab/scorecard.py` lines 75, 222 and 271).
   Locks written since 18 Nov therefore store `market_probability = null`.
3. **Effect:** the week's "beat the market" and "disagreed with market" counts
   for basketball are not representative. If the billing month is the calendar
   month that `_days_left` assumes, the gap lasts until 1 December. That covers
   Thanksgiving week's tournaments, which have the highest share of neutral
   sites. Football was mostly unaffected, because Saturday prices were bought
   on 17 and 22 Nov.

## Lessons

1. **Judge football on CLV and disagreements, not on hit rate.** The best record
   of the year came in a week when the market's edge grew. Nothing in football
   calls for a change this week.
2. **Basketball's early-season home edge is real and growing.** It is the
   biggest measurable loss in the record right now. The fix should come through
   the experiment process, not a direct edit.
3. **The scorecard's market column is only as complete as the paid feed.** It
   should fall back to the same ESPN lines the forecasts already use, taking
   only snapshots stored before the deadline, as `_market_at` already requires.
   That change is in the engine. Whether to fill locks already written since
   18 Nov from pre-deadline ESPN snapshots is a separate question for the
   owner, because locks are described as never rewritten.
4. **Odds pacing needs a per-day cap, not a per-cycle one.** Without one, the
   free 500 credits will run out mid-month again in December.

## Caveats

- One week is a small sample: 72 football games, 352 basketball games, and 47
  priced basketball games. The basketball home-edge finding rests on the season
  trend, not on this week alone.
- Home and away come from the event title. Early-season neutral-site games
  listed as "at" blur the split.
- The scorecard on `main` is the published record. The self-heal pass may
  correct individual rows afterwards.
