Privacy policy

Matchday does not require an account, payment information, or a real name to use its current local-first version. We do not run advertising trackers or sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app uses your browser’s local and session storage to remember language, selected sport, appearance settings, favourite team, watchlist, dismissed welcome screen, and your personal “Beat the Model” picks and results. A randomly generated device identifier and an optional display handle may also be stored locally for the community feature. This information remains in your browser unless you enable a future online leaderboard or clear site data.

Information sent to other services

The app loads sports data prepared by Matchday and may load Google Fonts. When you open a news story or an external data-provider link, that third party receives information under its own privacy policy. Hosting providers may also process ordinary technical logs, such as IP address, browser type, and request time, to operate and secure a hosted site.

Online leaderboard

It is disabled in the current release. If it is enabled later, Matchday would send the pseudonymous device identifier, optional handle, and aggregated pick statistics needed to operate the leaderboard. Before enabling it publicly, the publisher will update this page with the host, retention period, and contact details for that service.

Your choices

You can clear locally stored Matchday information through your browser’s site-data controls. This policy will be updated before any accounts, payments, marketing analytics, or public community profiles are introduced.

Responsible use

Do not misuse the service, interfere with it, attempt to access non-public systems, or use it where prohibited. If you use sports information in connection with gambling, follow local law and age restrictions in your jurisdiction — see the section below if that stops feeling fun.

Responsible gambling

Matchday doesn't take bets, doesn't get paid by anyone who does, and nothing on this site is written to talk you into placing one. What you do with the information is entirely your call. If you do gamble, the section below is worth reading either way — and worth sharing with someone else if it applies to them instead.

Some signs it's time to step back: chasing losses, betting more than you meant to, hiding it from people close to you, borrowing money to keep going, or spending money set aside for bills or savings.

Free, confidential help

United States — Call or text the National Problem Gambling Helpline at 1-800-522-4700, available 24/7, or visit ncpgambling.org for live chat and state-by-state resources. Gamblers Anonymous runs free peer-support meetings nationwide.

United Kingdom — GamCare's National Gambling Helpline is free and confidential at 0808 8020 133. BeGambleAware has self-assessment tools, support, and a self-exclusion directory.

Elsewhere — most jurisdictions with legal sports betting run a free, confidential national helpline; searching “problem gambling helpline” plus your country is usually the fastest way to find it.

Matchday is not a gambling operator and can't process self-exclusion requests, deposit limits, or account closures — those live with whichever sportsbook or platform you use. The organizations above can point you to the right tool, including national self-exclusion registers.
